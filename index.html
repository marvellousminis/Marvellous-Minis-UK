How It
Works

A straightforward commission process designed to keep you informed from the initial idea through to the finished miniature.

Tell Me What You Want

Send your model details, reference images, colour scheme and the level of finish you're looking for.

Receive Your Quote

You'll receive a clear estimate based on the model, condition, complexity and requested finish.

Painting Begins

Once the commission is agreed, your model is prepared, painted and finished to the agreed specification.

Quality Check

The finished miniature is carefully inspected before being photographed and prepared for return.

Delivered Safely

Your completed miniature is securely packaged for delivery, ready for the tabletop or display cabinet.

Enjoy Your Mini

Put your newly painted miniature on the battlefield, in the cabinet, or wherever it belongs.
