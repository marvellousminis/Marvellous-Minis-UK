THE BIG SLIDESHOW AT THE TOP OF THE WEBSITE
===========================================

Whatever photos are in this folder are the photos that rotate at the
top of the homepage. That's the whole rule.


TO ADD A PHOTO
--------------
1. Open this folder on github.com
2. Click "Add file" then "Upload files"
3. Drag your photo in (straight off your phone is fine)
4. Click "Commit changes"

Wait about two minutes, then reload the website. Done.

You do NOT need to convert anything. iPhone .HEIC photos are fine.
They get converted and shrunk automatically after you upload.


TO REMOVE A PHOTO
-----------------
1. Click the photo in this folder
2. Click the bin icon (top right)
3. Click "Commit changes"


TO CHANGE THE ORDER
-------------------
The photos play in number order, so rename them to reorder.

  1-titan-front.webp        <- shows first
  2-imperial-knight.webp    <- shows second
  3-...

To rename: click the photo, click the pencil icon, change the name
at the top, click "Commit changes".


HOW MANY?
---------
Four to six works well. Pick your strongest shots.
Close-up detail photos don't work here — they get cropped wide and
turn into a blur. Use them further down the page instead.


DON'T TOUCH
-----------
manifest.json — the website writes this one itself.
